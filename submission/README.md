# Signal Hoist: VAST Challenge 2023 answer sheets

**Entry Name:** Signal Hoist

This folder holds answer sheets in the VAST Challenge 2023 submission format for the three mini-challenges of the Oceanus / FishEye International scenario (illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing). It is an independent portfolio response written after the challenge, not an official 2023 entry.

- **Live interactive site:** <https://amukherjee1991.github.io/Vast23/>
- **Repository:** <https://github.com/amukherjee1991/Vast23>
- **Combined PDF:** [VAST2023-Signal-Hoist.pdf](VAST2023-Signal-Hoist.pdf)

## Answer sheets

| Sheet | Topic | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| [MC1.md](MC1.md) | Contextualising an NLP-extracted knowledge graph around four suspect entities | Q1–Q4 |
| [MC2.md](MC2.md) | Temporal trade patterns, judging 12 predicted-link bundles, companies that restart under new names | Q1–Q5 |
| [MC3.md](MC3.md) | Anomalous business groups, grouping similar businesses, an investigation list | Q1–Q5 |

Each answer stays within the challenge's word and image limits, states a confidence level for every finding, and cites its figures inline (Figure 1.3 is the third figure in Question 1's answer). The images in [`images/`](images/) are element screenshots of the live site's views at 1440 px width. The numbers come from the analysis pipelines in `pipeline/` and the findings in `findings/`.
